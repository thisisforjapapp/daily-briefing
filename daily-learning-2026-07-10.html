🔭 Astronomy & Cosmos

Humanity’s First Punch at the Cosmos: How NASA’s DART Mission (行星防禦測試) Crashed a Spacecraft into Asteroid Dimorphos — and Moved It Three Times Further Than Expected

🤔 Predict Before You Read
If you crash a 570-kilogram spacecraft into a 5-billion-kilogram asteroid at 6 km/s, how much do you expect the orbit to change — and what else might amplify the effect?
NASA’s DART spacecraft mission poster — the first planetary defence test in history. Credit: Wikimedia Commons / NASA

On 26 September 2022, at 23:14 UTC, a spacecraft the size of a golf cart travelling at 6.1 km/s slammed into Dimorphos (狄莫弗斯), a 160-metre moonlet orbiting the near-Earth asteroid (近地小行星) Didymos (迪迪莫斯). The impact was entirely deliberate — the culmination of NASA’s DART (Double Asteroid Redirection Test / 雙小行星重定向測試) mission, humanity’s first attempt to change the course of a natural space object. The Italian LICIACube cubesat watched as a spectacular plume of debris expanded into a comet-like tail thousands of kilometres long. Planetary defence (行星防禦) had moved from theory to proven fact.

Scientists expected the spacecraft to shift Dimorphos’s orbital period (公転週期) by roughly 10 minutes — their minimum success threshold. What ground-based radar and photometry measured astonished the team: the orbit shrank by 33 minutes, more than three times the target. The reason was momentum transfer amplification (動量轉移放大), or “ejecta enhancement.” DART’s impact excavated tonnes of rock and dust outward, acting as a secondary thruster in reverse — the escaping ejecta imparted additional recoil to the asteroid, multiplying the deflection far beyond the direct kinetic impact alone.

The mission targeted a binary system specifically because ground-based telescopes could precisely measure Dimorphos’s orbital period around Didymos — impossible against a single asteroid in open space. ESA’s Hera mission (赫拉任務), launched October 2024, is en route to image the crater and study long-term dynamics. The overarching lesson: the technology to deflect a hazardous asteroid exists, but only with years or decades of advance warning. Early detection — not last-minute heroics — is the real planetary defence strategy.

三千支火繩銃終結武田騎兵：織田信長如何以長篠之戰（1575年）改寫日本戰國的戰爭邏輯，開啟火器時代

🤔 閱讀前先猜猜
你面對當時日本最令人聞風喪膽的騎兵軍團，手下是步兵和火繩銃。裝填一次需時三十秒，騎兵衝鋒只需數秒。你會如何部署，才能讓銃砲真正發揮威力？
背景速覽

核心人物：織田信長（尾張國主）、德川家康（盟友）、武田勝賴（武田信玄之子）、馬場信房、山縣昌景（武田名將，戰死長篠）。

事件：天正三年（1575年）5月21日，長篠・設樂原之戰，織田・德川聯軍對陣武田軍，歷時約八小時。

時代背景：戰國後期，群雄並立。武田騎兵被譽為「天下無雙」，武田信玄生前令織田、德川屢嘗敗績；信長急需一場決定性勝利，打破武田神話，鞏固天下人的政治地位。

長篠之戰屏風圖（1575年）。來源：維基媒體共享資源

1575年，武田勝賴率約一萬五千大軍南下，將長篠城（今愛知縣新城市）團團封鎖，試圖奪取三河要地，與信長決一高下。武田騎兵素以機動突破著稱，「甲州流兵法」視騎馬衝鋒為核心戰術。然而信長已在堺（今大阪府）購入大批鐵砲，並苦思如何克服火繩銃裝填緩慢的致命弱點。他需要的不只是武器，而是一套全新的組織邏輯。

信長與德川家康的聯軍約三萬八千人，在設樂原沿連吾川構築長達數百公尺的木柵欄（馬防柵），以障礙物取代人牆抵擋騎兵。三千名鐵砲足輕分排部署，據後世史料及復原分析，採輪替射擊，確保銃聲不斷。5月21日清晨，武田名將接連率騎兵衝鋒，卻一波一波在銃聲中瓦解。馬場信房、山縣昌景等武田名將相繼陣亡，武田軍將士死亡估計超過一萬，勝賴狼狽撤退。「天下無雙」的武田騎兵神話，在數小時內煙消雲散。

長篠之戰的歷史意義，遠不止一場軍事勝利。它確立了一種新的戰爭邏輯：工事、組織、火力協同，取代了個人武勇與騎馬衝鋒。這與歐洲同期的「軍事革命」（Military Revolution）驚人相似——從騎士個人對決到有組織的火器步兵方陣，預示著近代國家軍事化的雛形。信長此後持續推廣鐵砲、推動商業立市（樂市樂座），加速了各地割據勢力的瓦解。武士道傳說繼續流傳，但長篠之後，真正的戰場主角已然易手。

🏛 History — Western

The Girl Who Lifted a Siege — and Invented a Nation: How Joan of Arc (聖女賙德) Led the Army of Orléans, Saw Charles VII Crowned, and Was Burned at the Stake Aged Nineteen

🤔 Predict Before You Read
A 17-year-old peasant girl claims God told her to lead the French army. What would it actually take for hardened medieval commanders to follow her into battle — and why might they?
Context

Key figures: Joan of Arc (聖女賙德, c.1412–1431), peasant from Domrémy; Dauphin Charles VII (查理七世), uncrowned king; Bishop Pierre Cauchon (科尚主教), trial judge; Count Dunois (鄧諾瓦伯爵), defender of Orléans.

Event at stake: Siege of Orléans (奧爾良之圍, 1428–1429), coronation of Charles VII at Reims (蓯良斯) July 1429, trial and execution at Rouen (盧昂) May 1431.

Big picture: The Hundred Years’ War (百年戰爭, 1337–1453) had left France shattered. English forces and Burgundian (勃良第) allies controlled Paris and northern France; the Treaty of Troyes (1420) had made the English king heir to France. Orléans was the last stronghold before the Loire — its fall would likely end French resistance.

Contemporary miniature of Joan of Arc, c. 1429 — the only near-contemporary image. Credit: Wikimedia Commons

By the winter of 1428, English and Burgundian forces had besieged Orléans (奧爾良) for six months. The city guarded the Loire Valley (盧瓦爸河谷) and the road south; its fall would likely end organised French resistance. Into this crisis walked Joan of Arc (聖女賙德), a teenager from Domrémy who claimed the voices of Saint Catherine, Saint Margaret, and Saint Michael had commanded her to drive out the English. Charles VII, desperate and sceptical, had her examined by theologians at Poitiers (普瓦捧) for three weeks. They found no heresy and no deception — and given his precarious position, Charles had little to lose by trying her.

Joan arrived at Orléans on 29 April 1429, carrying a white banner embroidered with the fleur-de-lis (鳢尾草百合花德國德). Within nine days, the six-month siege was broken — an achievement that had eluded trained commanders for months. Joan contributed tactical boldness, attacking the English bastille of Les Tourelles directly, but the morale transformation she catalysed was arguably more decisive. French troops who had retreated for years suddenly fought as if victory were inevitable. She then led the Loire Campaign, capturing four cities in six weeks, before escorting Charles to his coronation at Reims Cathedral (蓯良斯主教座) on 17 July 1429 — fulfilling exactly the two missions she had declared from the start.

In May 1430, Joan was captured by Burgundians near Compiègne (貢比涿) and sold to the English for 10,000 livres. Her trial at Rouen (盧昂) in 1431 was politically orchestrated: Bishop Cauchon used charges of heresy and cross-dressing to delegitimise both Joan and the coronation she had secured. She was burned at the stake aged approximately 19. Twenty-five years later, Pope Calixtus III ordered a retrial and overturned every verdict. What endures most profoundly is what her trial record reveals: she articulated loyalty not to a feudal lord’s land but to France as an idea — one of the earliest documented statements of national identity (民族認同) as a political force.

☯ 東方哲學

一燈萬影，萬影含一燈：唐代法藏如何以銅鏡廳向武則天演示華嚴宗「因陀羅網」的宇宙觀，開創中國佛教最壯闊的思想體系

🤔 閱讀前先猜猜
若萬物之間彼此互相映照、互相包含，「我」究竟在哪裏？「一」和「多」究竟是什麼關係？
背景速覽

核心人物：法藏（643–712年），法號賢首，華嚴宗三祖，祖籍粟特（今烏茲別克）；武則天（則天武後），中國唯一女皇。

核心思想：因陀羅網（帝釋天珠網，重重無盡映射）；六相圓融（總別同異成壞）；十玄門（一多相攝等十大命題）；事事無礙法界。

時代背景：武周時代，佛教被奉為國教，武則天以《大雲經》中「女菩薩轉世為王」之說鞏固政治合法性。法藏為武則天翻譯《華嚴經》，並以哲學體系支撐其政治需要，二者政治與學術利益高度契合。

大足石刻佛教雙塑（重慶，唐宋時期）。來源：維基媒體共享資源

約公元689年，法藏在洛陽宮廷請武則天進入一間他親手設計的廳室：八面銅鏡面面相對，正中懸一盞燭燈。燭火點亮，鏡中之燈影在鏡中的鏡影中無窮映射——每一面鏡子既映出其他七面，也映出其他七面所映的七面，直至無盡。法藏說：「此即《華嚴經》一多相攝之義，陛下之性德，亦如是也。」這不是魔術表演，而是一個精心設計的哲學示範：「因陀羅網」的核心命題——一即一切，一切即一——以感官體驗代替抽象論述，直指觀者心智。武則天據說當場豁然有悟。

《華嚴經》的「因陀羅網」比喻源自佛陀神話：帝釋天的宮殿懸有無窮寶網，每顆珠子各映所有其餘珠子，且每顆映像中又有更多映像，重重無盡。法藏將此比喻建構成嚴密的哲學體系：「六相圓融」論一切現象的總別、同異、成壞六個面向如何在不失各自差異的前提下相互圓融；「十玄門」進一步論證「一時具足相應門」、「廣狹自在無礙門」等十種命題，說明任何一法如何在不改變自身的情況下與所有其他法相互包攝。這既不是消除差異的「大同」，也不是各自封閉的「孤立」，而是差異中的圓融——法藏稱之為「事事無礙法界」。

華嚴宗的思想影響深遠而多向。禪宗雖反對繁瑣義學，其「一花一世界、一葉一如來」的美學意象幾乎直接源自因陀羅網的詩意。東傳日本後，華嚴宗以奈良東大寺（大佛殿）為本山，成為奈良佛教六宗之首，深刻塑造了日本中古文化中萬物相互滲透的美學感受。在當代，美國學者Francis Cook以「宇宙全息論（cosmic holography）」詮釋華嚴，而複雜系統理論中「湧現性（emergence）」的結構——整體特性無法從單一部分推論——與一多相攝有驚人的結構相似性。法藏的銅鏡廳，至今仍是哲學史上最優雅的思想演示之一。

The Man Who Invented Calculus — and Then Decided We Live in the Best of All Possible Worlds: Leibniz (萊布尼茲), the Monadology (單子論), and the Problem Voltaire (伏爾泰) Never Forgave Him For

🤔 Predict Before You Read
If God is all-powerful, all-knowing, and all-good — how do you explain earthquakes and war? And if mind and body are completely different substances, how does deciding to raise your arm actually move it?
Context

Key figures: Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (萊布尼茲, 1646–1716); René Descartes (笛卡兒, 1596–1650), whose mind-body dualism Leibniz inherited; Voltaire (伏爾泰, 1694–1778), whose Candide (1759) savaged Leibniz’s optimism.

Core ideas: Monads (單子) — windowless soul-like indivisible units; Pre-established harmony (前定和諧) — God synchronised all monads at creation; Theodicy (神正論) — this world is the best of all possible worlds (最完美的可能世界).

Big picture: Descartes had split the universe into mind (res cogitans) and matter (res extensa) but left no mechanism for interaction. Leibniz’s monadology was partly a solution to this mind-body problem (心身問題), partly a theodicy responding to the problem of evil (惡的問題).

Leibniz, painted by Christoph Bernhard Francke, c. 1695. Credit: Wikimedia Commons

In 1714, Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (萊布尼茲) wrote the Monadology (單子論), proposing that all reality consists of infinitely many indivisible units he called “monads” (單子). Unlike atoms, monads are not material — each is more like a centre of perception reflecting the entire universe from a unique vantage. Crucially, monads have “no windows”: they cannot directly affect one another. This seems strange until you recognise the problem Leibniz was solving. Descartes (笛卡兒) had split the universe into mind and matter — but offered no mechanism for how a mental decision to raise one’s arm causes muscles to contract. Leibniz’s answer was audacious: they don’t interact at all.

Leibniz’s solution was pre-established harmony (前定和諧). God, at the moment of creation, choreographed every monad so their internal states — unfolding entirely from within, like clockwork — would always correspond in perfect synchrony throughout all of time. Mind and body run like two perfectly calibrated clocks set in advance: they never touch, but they always agree. This device also furnished Leibniz’s theodicy (神正論): God chose our world from among all logically possible worlds (可能世界) because it achieves the optimal balance of simplicity in laws and richness in variety. Evil exists because eliminating it would require a more complicated or less rich world — this one is, all things considered, the best available.

This claim — that we live in “the best of all possible worlds” — became one of philosophy’s most ridiculed propositions. Voltaire’s Candide (1759) was a 200-page satirical demolition, written partly in the wake of the Lisbon earthquake (顸斯本大地震) of 1755. The novel’s relentlessly optimistic Dr. Pangloss destroyed Leibniz’s popular reputation. Yet philosophers take Leibniz more seriously: his “possible worlds” framework became foundational for 20th-century modal logic (模態遟輯) via Saul Kripke (克里普克) and David Lewis (大衛劳易斯). His calculus (微積分) notation remains in use today, and his vision of a universal logical calculus prefigures modern symbolic logic (符號遟輯).

🧠 Psychology — 🎯 Behavioral Economics

Same Choice, Different Answer: How the Framing Effect (框架效應) Proves That “400 Will Die” Feels Worse Than “200 Will Be Saved” — Even When the Mathematics Are Identical

🤔 Predict Before You Read
A surgeon tells you this operation has a “90% survival rate.” Another surgeon describes the same operation as having a “10% mortality rate.” Do you feel differently? Why, or why not?
Amos Tversky (特沃斯基), who co-discovered the Framing Effect with Daniel Kahneman. Credit: Wikimedia Commons

In 1981, psychologists Amos Tversky (特沃斯基) and Daniel Kahneman (卡尼曼) published a landmark experiment in Science that challenged the economic model of rational decision-making. They told participants to imagine an unusual Asian disease expected to kill 600 people. One group chose between: “Programme A saves 200 people” or “Programme B — one-third chance of saving all 600, two-thirds chance of saving no one.” A second group faced identical mathematical options reframed as deaths: “Programme C — 400 people will die” or “Programme D — one-third chance nobody dies, two-thirds chance all 600 die.” The numbers were identical. The choices were not.

The results were striking. When framed as gains (救人), 72% of participants chose the certain option (Programme A, 200 saved). When framed as losses (死亡), 78% switched to the risky option (Programme D). Prospect theory (前景理論) — which Tversky and Kahneman had introduced in 1979, later earning Kahneman the 2002 Nobel Prize in Economics (Tversky died in 1996) — predicts exactly this: people are risk-averse (風險規避) in the domain of gains, and risk-seeking (風險偏好) in the domain of losses. A rational decision-maker should be entirely unaffected by labelling. Real humans are systematically not.

The framing effect (框架效應) has been replicated across cultures, professions, and demographics. Surgeons, lawyers, and financial advisers all show measurable framing sensitivity. In medical communication (醫療溝通), patients presented with “90% survival rate” statistics agree to surgery at significantly higher rates than patients shown “10% mortality rate” — even when doctors note the equivalence explicitly. The practical antidote is simple but underused: before any important decision, deliberately reframe the choice in the opposite direction. If it feels different, your first reaction was driven by the frame, not the evidence.

🫀 Physiology

The Gas That Saved Millions: How Nitric Oxide (一氧化氮) — a Pollutant With a Five-Second Lifespan — Turned Out to Be One of the Body’s Most Powerful Signalling Molecules (信號分子)

🤔 Predict Before You Read
A gas produced in your blood vessel walls, surviving for only a few seconds, controls your blood pressure, prevents blood clots, explains how erectile function works, and makes beetroot juice enhance athletic performance. How is that possible?
The nitric oxide (NO) molecule — one nitrogen, one oxygen, enormous physiological power. Credit: Wikimedia Commons

For most of the 20th century, scientists assumed biologically important chemical messengers had to be stable, complex molecules — proteins or hormones durable enough to travel through the body. In 1980, pharmacologist Robert Furchgott (弗奇戈特) discovered that intact endothelium (內皮細胞) was required for acetylcholine to relax vascular smooth muscle, implying the endothelium secreted a mystery signal he called “endothelium-derived relaxing factor” (EDRF, 內皮舢弛因子). Seven years later, EDRF was identified as nitric oxide (一氧化氮, NO), a diatomic gas better known as an atmospheric pollutant from car exhausts. This earned Furchgott, Louis Ignarro (伊格納羅), and Ferid Murad (穆拉德) the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 1998.

Nitric oxide is produced from the amino acid L-arginine (左旋第精氨酸) by nitric oxide synthases (一氧化氮合齄, NOS). In the endothelium, eNOS is activated by shear stress from blood flow and exercise — making NO a key mechanism by which regular aerobic activity (有氧運動) lowers resting blood pressure. Once released, NO diffuses into adjacent smooth muscle cells, activating guanylate cyclase (鳥苷酸環化齄), raising cyclic GMP (環磷酸鳥苷苷, cGMP), and triggering vasodilation (血管舢弛). NO also inhibits platelet aggregation (血小板聚集), making it a cornerstone of cardiovascular homeostasis (心血管穩態).

Clinical applications are extraordinary. Sildenafil (西地那非) — marketed as Viagra — works by inhibiting PDE5 (第五型磷酸二酯戰), the enzyme that degrades cGMP; prolonging cGMP prolongs the vasodilatory effect of NO in penile tissue. Dietary nitrates (膜食硝酸焚) in beetroot (甜菜根) are converted to NO in the mouth by bacteria; this explains why beetroot juice supplementation measurably reduces the oxygen cost of submaximal exercise (Lansley et al. 2011). Inhaled NO (吸入性一氧化氮) is used in newborns with pulmonary hypertension (肊動脈高壓) to selectively dilate pulmonary vasculature. One molecule, a half-life of seconds, and a therapeutic footprint spanning cardiology, urology, neonatology, and sports science.

The Slow Fire Inside: How Chronic Low-Grade Inflammation (慢性低度發炎) Silently Drives Heart Disease, Diabetes, and Cognitive Decline — and What Two Landmark Trials Proved About Treating It Directly

🤔 Predict Before You Read
If a drug that lowers inflammation — not cholesterol — in people with normal cholesterol still reduces heart attacks significantly, what does that imply about what’s really causing the disease?
C-reactive protein (CRP, C反應蛋白) — the key blood biomarker of systemic inflammation. Credit: Wikimedia Commons / PDB

Inflammation (發炎) is designed to be brief and targeted. But when low levels of inflammatory signalling persist chronically without an active pathogen or injury, it becomes corrosive. Chronic low-grade inflammation (慢性低度發炎) is now linked to cardiovascular disease (心血管疾病), type 2 diabetes (二型糧尿病), depression (抑鬱症), Alzheimer’s disease (阿茲海默症), and certain cancers. Its signature blood biomarker (生物標誌物) is high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (高敏感性C反應蛋白, hsCRP), a liver-produced protein whose blood levels rise in proportion to systemic inflammatory activity. An hsCRP above 2 mg/L in an apparently healthy adult is a significant independent predictor of future cardiovascular events — distinct from cholesterol.

The JUPITER trial (Ridker et al., 2008, NEJM) enrolled 17,802 apparently healthy adults with normal LDL cholesterol (低密度脂蜗蛋白膽固醇) but elevated hsCRP (≥2 mg/L). Half received rosuvastatin (羅蘇伐他汀). The statin group experienced 44% fewer major cardiovascular events — dramatic enough that the data monitoring board stopped the trial early. Crucially, the benefit emerged even in participants without elevated cholesterol, suggesting statins’ anti-inflammatory properties (消炎特性) drive meaningful protection. The subsequent CANTOS trial (Ridker et al., 2017) went further: it used canakinumab, a targeted anti-inflammatory monoclonal antibody, achieving a 15% reduction in major cardiovascular events with no change in cholesterol. Inflammation is not a marker — it is a causal pathway.

The most modifiable drivers of chronic low-grade inflammation are lifestyle factors. Visceral fat (內臟脂肪) — packed around abdominal organs — functions as an endocrine organ (內分泸器官), secreting IL-6 (介白素-6), TNF-α (腫瑞壞死因子), and other pro-inflammatory cytokines (促炎細胞因子) even without infection. Ultra-processed food (超加工食品), smoking, sleep deprivation, and chronic stress all elevate hsCRP; the Mediterranean diet (地中海飲食) — demonstrated in the PREDIMED trial (Estruch et al., 2013, n=7,447) to reduce major cardiovascular events by 30% — and regular aerobic exercise robustly lower it. Inflammation is where lifestyle and chronic disease intersect, and unlike LDL it responds to behavioural change within weeks.

How Fast Do You Actually Lose Your Fitness? The Science of Detraining (去訓練效應) — and Why Aerobic Fitness (有氧體能) and Strength (肌力) Fade at Completely Different Rates

🤔 Predict Before You Read
If you stop exercising completely for four weeks due to travel or illness, how much of your aerobic fitness do you estimate you’ll lose? And do you think strength fades faster or slower than cardio?
Muscle atrophy (肌肉萎縮) from disuse — one component of detraining. Credit: Wikimedia Commons / OpenStax

Detraining (去訓練效應) — the partial reversal of training-induced adaptations when exercise ceases — follows a predictable pattern. The bad news for endurance athletes is immediate: aerobic capacity (有氧能力) drops rapidly. VO₂max (最大攝氧量) can fall 6–20% in just three to four weeks of complete inactivity (Mujika & Padilla, 2000, Sports Medicine). The primary mechanism is cardiovascular: plasma volume (血漿容量) — which expands roughly 10% with regular endurance training — begins contracting within 48–72 hours of the last session. As plasma volume falls, stroke volume (每搏輸出量) declines, and VO₂max follows. Highly trained athletes lose proportionally more in the first weeks, precisely because they had more plasma volume to lose.

Strength adaptations (肌力適應) are far more resilient. Substantial strength is maintained for approximately four to eight weeks of complete inactivity, and even after twelve weeks, losses typically amount to only 10–15% (Bosquet et al., 2013). Strength gains comprise two components. Neural adaptations (神經適應) — improved motor unit recruitment (運動單元招募) and intermuscular coordination — are acquired quickly but also lost slowly, persisting well beyond when training has stopped. Muscular hypertrophy (肌肉肖大) does eventually decline through reduced myofibrillar protein synthesis (肌原纖維蛋白合成) and fibre atrophy, but this requires many weeks of complete inactivity to produce measurable functional loss.

The most important practical finding concerns maintenance strategies. Mujika & Padilla (2000) showed that reducing training volume by as much as 60–90% while preserving training frequency and intensity maintained performance for up to four weeks. Even a single strength session per week at near-maximal intensity is sufficient to preserve most strength for six weeks (Bickel et al., 2011, Journal of Physiology). For endurance, two high-intensity interval sessions (高強度間歇) per week can maintain VO₂max for three weeks. The concept of “muscle memory” (肌肉記憶) — specifically the persistence of myonuclei (肌細胞核) donated by satellite cells (衛星細胞) during training — explains why retrained individuals regain fitness significantly faster than genuine novices. The gains are not truly lost; they are waiting to be recalled.

📈 Investing & Economic Thinking

The Formula That Priced Risk — and Then Revealed Its Own Flaw: The Black–Scholes Model (布萊克–休斯模型), the 1987 Crash, and the Volatility Smile (波動率微笑) That Shouldn’t Exist

🤔 Predict Before You Read
If you could mathematically calculate what an option (選擇權) is worth at any moment, what assumptions would you have to make about financial markets — and what happens if those assumptions are wrong?
Black–Scholes option price surface with random paths — the model that transformed derivatives markets. Credit: Wikimedia Commons

In 1973, economists Fischer Black (費舍爾布萊克) and Myron Scholes (邁龙休斯), building on work with Robert Merton (羅伯特默頓), published a formula for pricing options (選擇權) — contracts giving the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an asset at a predetermined price. Their insight: an option’s payoff could be replicated by continuously adjusting a portfolio of the underlying asset and risk-free bonds (無風險債券), enabling risk-neutral pricing (風險中性定價) without requiring any assumption about the market’s expected return. The model needed only five inputs: current price, strike price, time to expiry, risk-free rate, and — critically — volatility (波動率). Scholes and Merton received the Nobel Prize in Economics in 1997; Black had died in 1995.

The model transformed finance. For the first time, derivatives (衍生品) could be priced theoretically rather than purely by negotiation. But Black–Scholes rests on assumptions real markets violate: normally distributed returns (常態分佈報酬), constant and predictable volatility, continuous trading, and no sudden price jumps. Extreme moves occur far more frequently in practice. On Black Monday, 19 October 1987, the Dow Jones (道瓊斯工業平均指數) fell 22.6% in a single day — roughly 20 standard deviations below the expected range, a probability the model treated as essentially zero. After 1987, traders noticed something strange in options market prices.

The post-1987 anomaly is called the volatility smile (波動率微笑) or volatility skew (波動率偏斜). Under Black–Scholes, implied volatility (隐含波動率) should be constant across all strike prices. Instead, deep out-of-the-money put options (看跌期權) command systematically higher implied volatility than at-the-money options, reflecting the market’s post-1987 pricing-in of fat-tailed crash risk (尾部風險) the model ignores. This scar on the volatility surface has never disappeared. LTCM’s 1998 collapse — involving Scholes and Merton themselves — was partly attributable to assuming stable correlations that collapsed under stress. Black–Scholes remains the industry benchmark, but practitioners use it as a reference scaffold, layering in jump-diffusion models (跳趍擴散模型) and stochastic volatility (隨機波動率) to handle the reality the original formula denies.

⚖️ Political Economics — Theory in the Wild

Can a Government Really Never Run Out of Money? Modern Monetary Theory (現代貨幣理論) and the 2021–2022 Inflation Test

Sovereign currency issuers operate under different fiscal constraints than households — the central claim of MMT. Credit: Wikimedia Commons
🌍 Event
In 2020–2021, the US government deployed approximately $5 trillion in pandemic relief (疫情救助), funded through Treasury issuance absorbed by the Federal Reserve (聯邦儲備) — creating money at a scale that would have seemed irresponsible by prior orthodoxy. MMT proponents pointed to the absence of immediate fiscal crisis as partial vindication. Then in 2021–2022, US CPI inflation (消費者物價指數) reached 9.1% — the highest in 40 years.
📖 Theory
MMT (現代貨幣理論), developed by Warren Mosler, Randall Wray (倫道夫雷), and popularised by Stephanie Kelton (凱爪頓) in The Deficit Myth (2020), argues that governments issuing their own sovereign currency (主權貨幣) cannot involuntarily default. Spending creates money; taxation destroys it. The budget deficit is an accounting residual, not a financial constraint. The real constraint on spending is inflation (通貨膨輹): public expenditure becomes destabilising only when it exceeds the economy’s productive capacity (生產能力). Taxation is therefore an inflation-control instrument, not a revenue-raising device.
⚠️ Anomaly
The 2021–2022 inflation surge appeared to confirm the MMT inflation constraint but complicated its policy prescription. If the constraint is real, the response — raising taxes or cutting spending — is politically near-impossible at speed. The Fed instead raised rates sharply. MMT proponents argued the inflation was supply-driven (supply chain disruption, energy shocks) rather than demand-driven (excess money creation) and that monetary policy was the wrong tool. The causal attribution remains empirically contested.
🧪 Challenger
Orthodox frameworks accept that inflation is the real constraint but argue that identifying the threshold in real time is politically and technically impossible — making the MMT inflation-management prescription functionally unenforceable in democratic systems. Blanchard & Summers (2022, IMF) suggest the pandemic proved fiscal space is real but bounded by credibility, not theoretical solvency. Open question: can any democratic government reliably tighten fiscal policy fast enough to suppress inflation once populations have normalised fiscal expansion as entitlement?
💡 MMT correctly identifies that currency-issuing sovereigns face no solvency constraint — but the inflation ceiling is real, fast-moving, and politically near-impossible to enforce through the fiscal instruments MMT prescribes.

Cards 1 & 6 — Planetary Defence and the Framing Problem: DART’s success proved that a small nudge can have an outsized effect — the ejecta amplified the impact three times beyond the direct push.
