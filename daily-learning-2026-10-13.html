Astronomy & Cosmos

Reading the Gaps Around a Star

Predict before you read

Can a picture of dust reveal a planet that the picture does not show?

Actual telescope data of the dust belts; the orange colour represents infrared (紅外線) measurements, not the view of a human eye. NASA (美國太空總署), ESA (歐洲太空總署), CSA (加拿大太空總署); A. Pagan (阿莉莎帕根), Space Telescope Science Institute (太空望遠鏡科學研究所); A. Gáspár (安德拉什加斯帕爾), University of Arizona (亞利桑那大學). Image attribution and licence

The orange rings are not a photograph of a solid track. They are the infrared (紅外線) glow of dust around Fomalhaut (北落師門), recorded by the James Webb Space Telescope (詹姆斯韋布太空望遠鏡). Look for the dark spaces between brighter belts: the absence of material is part of the evidence. In observations reported in 2023, the telescope resolved inner structure that earlier observations had missed. The interesting question is not simply how many rings appear, but what keeps debris arranged rather than spread smoothly across the system.

A debris disk (碎屑盤) forms after a young system's original gas has largely dispersed. Collisions between larger bodies continually supply smaller particles. Those particles absorb starlight and emit heat, allowing a telescope to detect matter too small to image individually. A planet's gravity (引力) can redirect or remove orbiting material and help maintain edges and gaps. Researchers therefore compare the observed architecture with models containing unseen planets. The rings are clues to possible causes; they are not themselves separate detections of those planets.

This is an inverse problem (逆問題): start with an outcome and ask what could have produced it. Several arrangements may fit an incomplete observation. The discovery paper described a possible planet shaping the intermediate belt, rather than announcing a confirmed planet there. A useful next test must distinguish alternatives, through additional observations or tighter modelling. Enjoy the image as a visible record of ongoing collisions, while keeping two statements apart: the telescope resolved dusty structure; the particular hidden objects responsible remain an inference. That distinction preserves the wonder and the uncertainty together.

An unseen cause becomes convincing when it survives a test beyond the pattern that suggested it.
Why is a gap suggestive of a planet without being a direct planet detection?
Check your answer
Gravity can sculpt debris, but the image measures glowing dust. A proposed planet is one explanation of that structure and needs additional discriminating evidence.
Sources: NASA (美國太空總署): the dust-belt observations · Gáspár (加斯帕爾) and colleagues: the original imaging study
東方歷史

一座寶庫，怎樣把日用品變成歷史證據？

閱讀前先想一想

一件古物保存得完整，是否就能告訴我們整個社會怎樣生活？

奈良時代的聖武天皇與光明皇后，是正倉院收藏的重要起點。八世紀的日本朝廷以佛教護國，東大寺既是宗教中心，也承接皇室供獻。這裏要看的不只是珍寶，而是物件、清單與保管制度如何共同留下可核對的過去。
正倉院校倉建築。現存寶物由現代庫房保管；照片中的木屋不是今天的全部保存系統。 圖片來源及授權

公元756年，光明皇后把已故聖武天皇生前珍愛的物品獻給東大寺大佛。正倉院保存的，不全是為後人製作的紀念品；書寫用具、樂器、衣服、藥物和器皿，把皇室生活的具體細節留了下來。宮內廳的館藏介紹指出，供獻清單與物件上的銘記可以互相核對。這是一個有文獻與實物支持的起點，與只靠後世傳說描述一位皇帝的喜好，證據性質不同。

物件能活過漫長歲月，背後也有人為的管理。庫門受封印與開閉權限約束，取出和歸還並非任人處置；延續的保管，讓部分物件的來歷比出土後才辨認的古物更容易追索。三角木材構成的校倉建築固然醒目，但不能把保存成果全歸功於一種「會呼吸」的木屋。今天寶物已移到現代庫房；建築、紀錄、修復和管理，是不同層面的保護，不能由照片中的外形代替整套解釋。

收藏還有另一個用途：它保留跨地域文化交流的痕跡。材料與工藝涉及中國、東南亞、伊朗等地，某些紋樣也呈現更遠的風格聯繫。不過，看到異地風格，不等於證明那件物品完整走過一條固定路線，更不等於奈良普通人都享有同樣生活。皇室與寺院的留存有明顯選擇性。正倉院讓我們更細緻地看見過去，同時提醒我們：保存下來的世界，是歷史的一扇窗，並不是整座房屋。

古物的說服力，來自物件與紀錄能否互相核對；珍貴不等於具有代表性。
為何清單和保管紀錄，會改變一件古物能支持甚麼歷史判斷？
揭示答案
清單、銘記與持續保管有助確定年代、用途和來歷；但皇室收藏仍不能直接代表整個社會。
資料：日本宮內廳：正倉院的沿革、寶物與保管 · 日本宮內廳：正倉院與供獻物品介紹
History — Western

The Sewer That Moved a Problem

Predict before you read

Does carrying sewage out of a city mean the pollution problem has been solved?

In nineteenth-century London (倫敦), population growth strained waste disposal. Joseph Bazalgette (約瑟夫巴澤爾傑特), chief engineer of the Metropolitan Board of Works (都會工程委員會), organised an enormous sewer project after the Great Stink (大惡臭) of 1858 made the river crisis politically unavoidable.
An 1858 cartoon depicts death on the polluted river. It records contemporary criticism, not a measured account of disease transmission. Image attribution and licence

The River Thames (泰晤士河) carried human and industrial waste past the buildings where lawmakers worked. During the hot summer of 1858, the smell reached Parliament (英國國會), and funding for a major sewer scheme followed. This sequence is documented; it does not require imagining a private conversation or deciding that every legislator had the same motive. The revealing detail is the geography: a problem that had long burdened residents became difficult for the people approving expenditure to keep at a distance.

Bazalgette's network connected existing drains to intercepting sewers (截流渠), then used pumping stations to carry waste farther downstream. This changed the route through which a densely populated city encountered its own sewage. Streets, riverbanks and underground tunnels became parts of a coordinated system, rather than isolated household arrangements. It also required organisation, financing and many workers; attributing the achievement to a lone engineering genius hides the institution that made construction possible. The system's official opening and the completion of the whole project were separate stages.

The uncomfortable limit is that moving waste was not the same as treating it. The original scheme discharged sewage into the lower river. The London Museum (倫敦博物館) describes how the 1878 sinking of the Princess Alice (愛麗絲公主號) exposed people to polluted water near those discharges and helped drive treatment improvements. The wider lesson is not that the sewers failed: they changed exposure within the city. It is that a successful intervention can redistribute harm, leaving another problem for later institutions to recognise and address.

Follow a problem to its new destination before calling it solved.
What did interception accomplish, and what did it leave unresolved?
Check your answer
It diverted sewage away from central urban exposure and carried it downstream. Discharge still polluted the receiving river, so treatment remained a separate task.
Sources: London Museum: construction, discharge and subsequent limits · Geological Society of London (倫敦地質學會): the sanitation crisis
東方哲學

厚葬真的是孝嗎？先問誰承擔代價

閱讀前先想一想

如果一種禮俗表達敬意，是否就足以證明它值得全社會遵行？

戰國時代的墨家關心戰爭、貧困與公共秩序。《墨子》的節葬論，不只是勸人省錢，而是挑戰一項倫理判準：對死者的敬意，能否不問對生者造成的影響？文本反映學派的論辯，不是每場古代葬禮的實錄。
後世對墨子的描繪，並非有可考據依據的生前肖像。 圖片來源及授權
🔗 延伸自「墨子止楚攻宋」：今次聚焦節葬如何把禮俗的公共代價納入道德判斷。

想像一項禮俗要求家庭大量耗費財物，又長時間停止日常工作。這是用來理解問題的假設，並非重構某一場葬禮。墨家對厚葬久喪的批評，正要把禮俗背後的成本放回眼前：活着的人還需要食物、照顧與安定生活。如果判斷只停在「看來夠不夠尊敬」，就容易把被犧牲的需要隱藏起來。節葬篇要求考察一種慣例能否改善貧困、人口與秩序，而不是只看其聲望。

這種推理把道德評價的重心放在實際後果，但不是把一切換算成一個金額。墨家的「利」包括物質生活、社會安定與家庭延續等多項善。它首先評估一套普遍做法，問大家長期遵行會帶來甚麼，而不是只判斷一次行為是否令人感動。當厚葬被當作孝的必要證明，學派便追問：這個標準真的幫到人嗎？倘若造成匱乏，原本值得稱許的名義，也不能自動替結果開脫。

論點的限制同樣重要。儀式可以承載哀傷、記憶和關係；若只把這些價值當成沒有用途的消耗，評估就可能過窄。斯坦福哲學百科的解讀也指出，墨家重視家庭角色與德行，不能簡化成反對親情，或要求對所有人做一模一樣的事。這場爭論值得保留的，是一個公開可問的問題：我們是在照顧所珍惜的關係，還是在維持一種昂貴的證明方式？回答仍須容納多種人的需要。

敬意的名義不能代替後果的檢查；成本也不能代替全部價值。
墨家為何不是單純主張「最便宜的禮俗最好」？
揭示答案
它以民生、人口與秩序等多項善評估慣例，並非只看價格；但如何充分理解儀式的情感與關係價值，仍是重要難題。
資料：斯坦福哲學百科：墨家倫理與節葬論
Philosophy — Western

Who Gets Taught to Think?

Predict before you read

If people have little practice in reasoning, does their hesitation establish that they lack the capacity?

Mary Astell (瑪麗阿斯特爾), writing in England (英格蘭) around the turn of the eighteenth century, proposed serious education for women. Her project grew from a religious and rationalist (理性主義) philosophy, rather than a modern programme for equal employment.
Title page of her educational proposal: a surviving publication, not evidence that the proposed institution was built. Image attribution and licence

Astell's question begins with an unfair comparison: judge minds that have received different opportunities as if they had received the same preparation. She argued that women encouraged to care mainly about appearance and approval lacked a method for cultivating their understanding. In A Serious Proposal to the Ladies (《向女士們提出的鄭重建議》), she offered both an institution for study and a route to improvement outside it. The project challenged a circular judgement in which restricted education helped produce the limitations then cited to justify restricting education.

Her proposed remedy was a method (方法) for thought: learn to recognise confusion, attend carefully, and move towards clearer understanding rather than surrender to the first difficulty. The aim included governing oneself through reason instead of dependence on praise. This connected an account of the mind to a practical educational environment. A person can possess a capacity without having the conditions to exercise it well. In this argument, instruction and practice matter because a poor performance need not reveal the natural limit of the learner.

It would distort the history to turn Astell into a spokesperson for every present-day equality claim. Her educational audience and religious purposes were specific, and her wider politics did not simply match contemporary liberalism (自由主義). The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (斯坦福哲學百科) stresses that her programme was not modern career training. We can nevertheless examine the diagnostic distinction her work makes useful: when someone struggles, which part belongs to capacity, which to preparation, and which to the setting? A fair answer must investigate those possibilities rather than treating the observed outcome as its own explanation.

Before judging a capacity, ask what opportunity there has been to develop it.
Why can unequal preparation make a comparison of ability circular?
Check your answer
Restriction can produce poor performance, which is then used to justify the original restriction. The performance alone does not separate inherent capacity from educational conditions.
Sources: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Astell, sections 2.2 and 3
Psychology

The Alternative Missing From the Price Tag

Predict before you read

Would “do not buy” and “keep the money for something else” always feel like the same choice?

A shopping street illustrates the setting of spending decisions; this photograph is not one of the experiment sites. Image attribution and licence

A hypothetical purchase offers two answers: buy the item, or do not buy it. Add a few words to the second answer—keep the money for other purchases—and the financial possibilities remain unchanged. Yet Shane Frederick (沙恩弗雷德里克) and colleagues found that this small change affected stated choices in a 2009 experiment. Students imagined buying a video; those reminded about other uses of the money were less willing to buy. The evidence was a controlled choice task, not an anecdote proving that every shopper is careless.

The neglected comparison is opportunity cost (機會成本): the best alternative that a decision prevents you from taking. Attention (注意力) is limited, and a purchase display makes the focal object easy to imagine while leaving outside possibilities unspecified. The reminder does not create another budget; it changes what the existing budget brings to mind. This belongs to behavioural economics (行為經濟學) because formally equivalent choices can lead to different behaviour when their presentation changes. Knowing that money can be spent elsewhere is different from actively considering an elsewhere during the decision.

A cheaper choice is not automatically better. If the alternative is less valuable to you, buying can remain sensible after reflection. Nor should a hypothetical student experiment become a precise prediction of a household's annual spending. A later meta-analysis (元分析) examines how opportunity-cost reminders vary across studies and decision types. The useful practice is modest: name one credible alternative, including keeping flexibility, before deciding. That makes a missing comparison visible; it does not promise immunity to persuasion, nor require turning every small purchase into a lengthy calculation.

Ask what this choice displaces, then decide whether the trade is worthwhile.
Why can a reminder change a decision without changing its objective budget?
Check your answer
It makes an already available alternative salient. The resources and options remain the same, but the comparison the person actively considers changes.
Sources: Frederick and colleagues: Opportunity Cost Neglect (忽略機會成本), study 1A · Research synthesis of opportunity-cost reminders

How a Gland Moves Water

Predict before you read

Does a cell have to pump each water molecule individually to produce a fluid?

Major glands around the mouth and jaw; this is an anatomical illustration, not a view of cellular transport. Image attribution and licence

A mouthful of saliva (唾液) looks like simple water, but producing it is an organised transport problem. A salivary gland (唾液腺) must move fluid towards a duct (導管) rather than merely let everything drift in all directions. Its secretory cells (分泌細胞) form a boundary, with different machinery facing the blood supply and the duct space. That arrangement gives the cells a direction. The anatomy diagram shows the larger glands; the mechanism depends on processes far smaller than the structures the illustration can display.

Nerve signals activate receptors (受體), raising calcium (鈣) signals inside secretory cells. These signals regulate channels (通道) and transporters (轉運蛋白) that move ions (離子). Moving dissolved substances creates an osmotic gradient (滲透梯度), and water follows through available pathways. The gland therefore controls much of the fluid movement by controlling solute (溶質) movement. It does not need a separate motor carrying each water molecule. The 2014 review cited below explains that sustained secretion also requires continued calcium entry; a brief initial signal is not the whole process.

This mechanism is a coordinated system, not an invitation to increase saliva by consuming extra calcium. Dietary intake and a regulated signal inside a cell are different things. Molecular details have been studied using several experimental models, and different components contribute to different parts of the process. A broad explanation should preserve those limits rather than treating every gland as identical plumbing. The memorable foundation is the relationship between a barrier, a directional transport system and water: changing where dissolved particles are concentrated can change where fluid flows.

Water movement can be organised indirectly by moving dissolved particles.
What makes fluid secretion directional rather than a uniform leak?
Check your answer
Cells have differently organised surfaces and transport machinery. Coordinated ion movement creates gradients towards the duct, enabling directed water flow.
Sources: Ambudkar (安布德卡爾): calcium signals and salivary fluid secretion · Review: salivary gland function, development and regeneration

Why a Little Sugar Belongs in a Rehydration Solution (口服補液溶液)

Predict before you read

Could adding a nutrient help replace fluid without first stopping the illness causing the loss?

A prepared solution illustrates the treatment category. The image supplies no recipe or dosage. Image attribution and licence

During diarrhoea (腹瀉), the urgent problem can be the loss of water and electrolytes (電解質), even while the intestine continues to absorb some substances. Oral rehydration solution (口服補液溶液) combines glucose (葡萄糖) with salts in carefully chosen proportions. The sugar has a job beyond making the drink taste pleasant. Research in cholera (霍亂) helped establish that a particular absorptive pathway remained usable during severe watery diarrhoea. Treatment could exploit that remaining function rather than waiting for the intestine to return to normal before replacing what had been lost.

Sodium (鈉) and glucose can enter intestinal cells together through coupled transport (協同轉運). Their absorption supports water absorption across the gut lining. This is why the composition matters: the solution is a physiological tool, not simply “more sugar means more energy.” The World Health Organization (世界衞生組織) scientific report describes evidence that glucose-mediated absorption remained largely intact in cholera, while later guidance specifies balanced mixtures. Rehydration (補液) addresses the fluid deficit; it does not necessarily remove the cause of diarrhoea or immediately stop its symptoms.

The distinction is useful without turning the card into a homemade treatment recipe. Excessively concentrated sugar can hinder the intended balance, and different drinks are not automatically interchangeable with a formulated solution. Follow the product's mixing instructions and appropriate clinical guidance rather than improvising concentrations. The National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (美國國家糖尿病及消化與腎病研究所) notes that severe dehydration (脫水) may require hospital treatment and intravenous fluids (靜脈補液), and that infants need medical advice. The striking achievement is conditional: a surviving transport pathway can support treatment, but its usefulness does not make every illness manageable at home.

A treatment can exploit a function that still works without curing the process that damaged other functions.
Why is rehydration not the same claim as stopping diarrhoea?
Check your answer
It replaces lost water and electrolytes through a still usable absorption pathway. The cause and ongoing losses may need separate assessment and treatment.
Sources: WHO: scientific basis, introduction and intestinal transport · WHO: balanced rehydration salts and preparation · NIDDK: treatment and escalation limits
Sports Science

Before Calling Someone a Non-responder (無反應者)

Predict before you read

If two people improve by different amounts, have you measured a biological difference in their response?

A group training session illustrates shared exposure. The photo does not measure how any participant responded. Image attribution and licence

Two people follow the same training programme. One improves noticeably on a test, while the other's score barely moves. Calling the second person a non-responder (無反應者) feels descriptive, but it smuggles in a cause: the programme supposedly produced little adaptation in that individual. The measured change also includes ordinary day-to-day variation and measurement error (測量誤差). A difficult testing day can therefore resemble poor trainability (可訓練性). The observation is real; the explanation attached to it still needs evidence.

Researchers studying individual response variability (個體反應差異) need a comparison for the variation that would occur without the programme. A control group (對照組), or appropriate repeatability (重複測量可靠性) information, can help separate the spread of training-induced changes from background noise. Greg Atkinson (格雷格阿特金森) and Alan Batterham (艾倫巴特勒姆) explain why a colourful chart of individual before-and-after changes cannot by itself make that separation. A 2021 systematic review (系統綜述) further found that many studies classified responses without first adequately estimating genuine differences in trainability.

This is not proof that everyone responds equally. It is a demand for evidence before assigning durable labels. Response also belongs to a particular outcome, dose and observation period: little change on one test is not a verdict on every useful adaptation. For an individual interpreting a disappointing result, repeated comparable measurements and a review of actual training exposure provide better grounds than one dramatic comparison. Even a well-designed group study cannot reveal a person's unobserved alternative life with certainty. Honest measurement narrows the possibilities; it does not erase biological variation or uncertainty.

A difference between two measured changes is a starting question, not yet a diagnosis of different trainability.
Why is the spread of changes in the training group not enough?
Check your answer
It includes background variation and measurement error as well as training response. A comparator or suitable repeatability evidence is needed to estimate the extra variation attributable to training.
Sources: Atkinson and Batterham: true and false response differences · Bonafiglia (博納菲利亞) and colleagues: systematic review of response classification
Investing & Economic Thinking

One Fund, Two Kinds of Price

Predict before you read

Can the price of a fund share differ from the value of the assets behind it?

A stock exchange (證券交易所) illustrates where fund shares may trade; the building itself says nothing about a fund's value. Image attribution and licence

Suppose a fund's assets, after subtracting liabilities, are worth 100 currency units per share, but a buyer pays 101 on an exchange. This is a hypothetical example of a premium (溢價), not a report of today's prices. An exchange-traded fund (交易所買賣基金), or ETF, has a market price (市價) at which buyers and sellers trade. Its net asset value (資產淨值), or NAV, is a calculation based on the fund's holdings. These are related quantities with different meanings, and a trading price need not equal the calculation at every instant.

The connection is maintained partly through creation and redemption (增設與贖回). Authorised participants (認可參與者), typically large financial institutions, can exchange specified baskets of assets or cash for large blocks of fund shares, or reverse that transaction. When differences are worth exploiting, arbitrage (套戥) can help bring the traded price towards the underlying value. Ordinary small investors usually trade existing shares on an exchange; they do not personally perform this large-block exchange with the fund. The mechanism links two markets without making them a single market.

The Securities and Exchange Commission (美國證券交易委員會) describes price alignment as an expected result, not an identity guaranteed at all times. NAV is normally calculated at a specified daily time, while trades occur throughout the day, so comparing a current quote with an older valuation can itself mislead. Costs and trading conditions also matter. A fund trading below NAV is therefore not automatically free money, just as trading close to NAV does not remove the risks of its holdings. Understanding the plumbing helps distinguish asset risk from the particular price paid for access to those assets.

Check what a quoted price measures and when its comparison value was calculated.
Why can creation and redemption encourage alignment without guaranteeing exact equality?
Check your answer
They create incentives to exploit worthwhile differences, but trades and valuations have different timing and trading has costs. The mechanism influences prices rather than defining them as equal.
Sources: SEC Investor Bulletin: NAV, premiums, discounts and arbitrage
Political Economics

A Market That Matches Without a Price

Predict before you read

Could willing donors remain unable to help even when there are enough people willing to give?

Kidney anatomy illustrates the organ involved; it is not a compatibility chart or clinical eligibility guide. Image attribution and licence
EVENT
In kidney exchange (腎臟交換), a patient has a willing donor whose kidney is incompatible. Another pair may have the reverse compatibility. Exchanging donors can make both transplants possible. Alvin Roth (阿爾文羅斯) and colleagues helped design organised exchanges.
THEORY
Matching theory (配對理論) treats compatibility and participation as allocation constraints. A simple two-pair cycle works only when each donor can help the other patient. More willingness alone cannot replace a missing compatible link.
ANOMALY
A mathematically feasible cycle may be difficult to carry out: participants could withdraw, and hospitals must coordinate. This is a limit of a model that assumes its matches can be implemented, rather than a refutation of matching mathematics.
CHALLENGER
Market design (市場設計) adds practical rules. Simultaneous operations can protect participants in cycles; chains beginning with an unpaired donor allow other arrangements. Compatibility, logistics and consent remain constraints. Maximising a match count is not the same task as deciding every fairness question.
A feasible allocation needs rules that make participation and implementation workable.
Sources: Roth: Kidney Exchange, a researcher account · Ashlagi (阿什拉吉) and Roth: an operations perspective

Connection of the Day · Moving water by moving particles

Cards 7 and 8 describe a shared physical foundation: controlling dissolved substances can organise water movement across a barrier. Salivary secretion moves fluid towards a duct; intestinal absorption takes fluid into the body. Neither process is adequately explained by imagining that each water molecule receives its own instruction.

The analogy stops at that foundation. Different tissues, transporters and directions are involved, and oral rehydration depends on a particular pathway remaining functional during illness. A mechanism that explains secretion does not by itself establish a clinical treatment.
